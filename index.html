Practice assignments

  1. Find the department with the largest total expense overrun.
  2. Filter to March and West. Identify the largest unfavorable variance.
  3. Model a 5% expense reduction. Does your selection fall within budget?
  4. Export the filtered data and reconcile the totals in Excel.
  5. Write a three-sentence summary: result, likely questions to investigate, and next action. The data alone does not establish causes.

Definitions: expense variance = actual − budget; variance % = total variance ÷ total budget. Cost per transaction = total actual ÷ total transactions. Positive expense variance is unfavorable. Scenario expense = actual × (1 + adjustment / 100). Dataset is synthetic and contains no Walmart information.
