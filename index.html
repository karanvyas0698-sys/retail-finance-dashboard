Analyst practice questions
  1. Find the department with the largest total expense overrun.
  2. Filter to March and West. Identify the largest unfavorable variance.
  3. Model a 5% expense reduction. Does your selection fall within budget?
  4. Export the filtered data and reconcile the totals in Excel.
  5. Write a three-sentence summary: result, likely questions to investigate, and next action. The data alone does not establish causes.

Definitions: expense variance = actual − budget; variance % = total variance ÷ total budget × 100 (not an average of row percentages). Cost per transaction = total actual ÷ total transactions. A zero denominator displays N/A. Positive expense variance is unfavorable; negative expense variance is favorable; zero is on budget. Scenario expense = actual × (1 + adjustment / 100); scenarios never overwrite recorded actuals. All data is fictional and contains no Walmart information. These summaries do not establish why expenses changed. Saved scenario settings use this browser's local storage; availability may vary for local files. No external libraries or network connection required.
